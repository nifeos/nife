# RedLeaf: the appendices to notes/redleaf.md

[`notes/redleaf.md`](../redleaf.md) carries what RedLeaf is, the three bets it makes against this
tree with the conclusion of each, where it is ahead, what happened after 2020, and BUGS. These files
carry the readings, tables and audits its prose budget has no room for. All were written from
sources read on 2026-09-20.

- [`sources.md`](sources.md): the seven documents and one clone, where each was fetched, and what
  the reading could not establish.
- [`the-2017-ancestor.md`](the-2017-ancestor.md): Levy et al. (APSys '17) in full, the three
  questions it left open, and what this tree can and cannot say in its terms.
- [`corrections-to-the-brief.md`](corrections-to-the-brief.md): the maintainer's sketch, claim by
  claim, against the text.
- [`the-trusted-build.md`](the-trusted-build.md): RedLeaf's admissions, its trusted compilation
  environment, and the audit that found the safe-Rust rule unenforced.
- [`crossing-costs.md`](crossing-costs.md): RedLeaf's Table 1, nife's own figures as a chain of
  ratios, and Atmosphere's Table 3.
- [`where-redleaf-is-ahead.md`](where-redleaf-is-ahead.md): the four results with their figures,
  and the IDL observation.
- [`after-2020.md`](after-2020.md): the repository's state, the PLOS '21 critique, and Atmosphere.
