Every boot's last beat, at t=120 s, reads `refused=0 mismatch=0 stalled=0`, and every wakerate
kept up with radon's 400/s. Two draws never crossed cores: E16 and E22 held 19 and 23 crossings
from the first beat to the last, so the curve excludes them. The eleven that crossed add 218,216.

The tally (`script/board-console --tally`) put all thirteen on one clean core, at 182,096 to
197,384/s. Placement by that count was the same every time, yet crossings ranged from 19 to
27,502. So the clean-core count, which predicts the rate, does not predict how often the tick
waiters cross.
