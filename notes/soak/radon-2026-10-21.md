## The unexplained reset: E27

Draw 2 reset between its beat at t=105 s and t=110 s. The beat line ends in a carriage return and
line feed, and the next bytes are `U-Boot SPL`. None of the lines the kernel prints before its
reset appear (`window reached`, `Cold-rebooting in 5s`, the AXP15060 write), and the 120 s window
had not been reached. Nobody touched radon or its outlet (calef, 2026-10-21 UTC), nothing wrote to
the serial port, and radon has no watchdog armed. The other twenty-four resets each printed the
AXP15060 lines first. Its row reads `clean` by the table's rule, which looks only at the last beat;
the reset is recorded here and in milestone 225's `BUGS`, not as a soak defect, because nothing
yet ties it to the soak.
