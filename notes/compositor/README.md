# The compositor: the appendices to notes/compositor.md

[`notes/compositor.md`](../compositor.md) carries the shape, the identity-free doorbell, what a
frame is, focus, the two refusals, a summary of each proof, the missing primitive, the trust
boundary and the limits. These files carry the evidence its prose budget has no room for.

- [`wayland.md`](wayland.md): the difference in kind from Wayland, stated precisely.
- [`terminal-focus.md`](terminal-focus.md): two terminals and one keyboard, the test that cashed
  the focus claim, and the two things it taught.
- [`screen-sharing.md`](screen-sharing.md): why the capture client is the screen-sharing case.
- [`proofs.md`](proofs.md): the isolation test from four directions, the damage rectangle on host
  and guest, and the four witnesses to the picture.
- [`missing-primitive.md`](missing-primitive.md): what a wait-any or a shared address space would
  change.
- [`window-tearing.md`](window-tearing.md): why a window can tear, and why reads stay bounded.
- [`arithmetic.md`](arithmetic.md): why the scene is fixed and shaped as it is, the two clips, and
  the composite.
- [`kernel-wiring.md`](kernel-wiring.md): what `kernel/src/user/compositor_service.rs` hands the
  compositor and each client, and why the test wiring is shaped that way.
