# Citations: the appendices to notes/citations.md

[`notes/citations.md`](../citations.md) carries the convention, the tolerance rule, the ratchet,
the census, `--moved`, attributed quotations, the examples and every limitation in one sentence.
These files carry the evidence its prose budget has no room for.

- [`ratchet.md`](ratchet.md): why a ratchet on new text, what it cannot catch, and its measured
  cost.
- [`census-and-backfill.md`](census-and-backfill.md): the census table, how its zero was
  re-checked, and the rule that chose the 25 glosses of the backfill.
- [`moved.md`](moved.md): why `--moved` tells rather than obliges, the four measurements behind
  it, and its cost over history.
- [`another-medium.md`](another-medium.md): the same defect in a `// SAFETY:` comment.
- [`first-run.md`](first-run.md): what the gate found on its first run, each wrong record named.
- [`bugs.md`](bugs.md): every `BUGS` entry in full.

`notes/X.md`. calef names directories.*
