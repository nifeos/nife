# The command line as a grant expression: the appendices to notes/grant-expression.md

[`notes/grant-expression.md`](../grant-expression.md) carries the idea, the grammar, where the
authority comes from and how it moves, the refusals, per-file grants and their rules, `date`, and
the interrupt grant. These files carry the argument and the evidence its prose budget has no room
for.

- [`two-words.md`](two-words.md): why `run` and `file:` were dropped (milestone 47 (navigation
  and naming)), what the parser stopped doing, and the refusal ordering that changed with them.
- [`memory-grant.md`](memory-grant.md): why untyped had to become delegable, and the program that
  proves `--mem` moves real memory.
- [`per-file-grants.md`](per-file-grants.md): the verb table, why the caretaker is a process, how
  it is proven, the interactive prompt, what it cost, and `wc report.txt` as an input operand.
- [`clock-grant.md`](clock-grant.md): the four links `date` needed, the one this note got wrong,
  and what a delegable clock would still need.
- [`spawn-protocol.md`](spawn-protocol.md): the shell-to-progenitor exchange in order, the reasons
  behind its data messages, frames and sentinels, and its BUGS in full.
